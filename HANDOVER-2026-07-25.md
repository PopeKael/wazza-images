## Important current behaviour

The move operation is intentionally simple again: one direct non-overwriting `Move-Item`. Do not run multiple filer processes or overlapping PowerShell windows.

Some source files reported as missing because stale concurrent processes had already moved or affected them. Do not assume a failed analysis; inspect `Done`, `Undecided`, and the rollback CSV first.
